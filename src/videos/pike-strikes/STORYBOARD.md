# PikeStrikes storyboard

The sixty-second edit uses 22 shots. Early frames give the pike an imposing
point of view, the middle breaks its decision into visible cues, and the strike
accelerates through short, matched cuts. The last sequence restores distance.

| Start frame | Shot and rig | Picture and story beat |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Eye, macro dolly | A lure sweeps past an oversized pike eye. Pull focus into the weed-shadowed face in the opening second. |
| 60 | Reveal, orbit | Open up on “attack” and reveal the fish at the edge of the weed bed. |
| 169 | Cast, dolly | Warm shore and full figure. The angler draws back, releases, then follows through. |
| 206 | Surface, overhead | Catch the final leg of the cast. The lure lands and concentric ripples break the surface. |
| 220 | Entry, tracking | Match the falling lure below the surface. Bubbles rise while the camera follows the drop. |
| 266 | Weed, dolly with rack focus | Slide past reeds and shift focus from the vegetation to the ambush. |
| 387 | Wobble, macro | Let the lure roll across the light on “wobble” and “flash.” |
| 436 | Speed, tracking | A tilted move follows the changing retrieve cadence and places the fish behind the lure. |
| 486 | Pike POV | Watch through the pike’s eyeline. The plug and line read against the open water. |
| 619 | Wake, tracking | Move beside the lure so nearby suspended specks trace its wake. |
| 708 | Lateral line, macro dolly | Travel along the spotted flank to make the sensory organ’s position clear. |
| 859 | Pause, orbit with rack focus | The lure stops moving. Focus travels through the gap to the watcher. |
| 923 | Turn, overhead | Show the dorsal drawing and the pike changing heading in the lane. |
| 1004 | Follow, tracking | Hold both fish and lure in view. The fish follows without committing. |
| 1099 | Twitch, macro | The lure snaps out of its pause. Its line takes up slack. |
| 1156 | Bend, tail insert | The tail loads and the flank flexes as the fish prepares to move. |
| 1186 | Drive, overhead | Keep the direction of travel as the tail kicks and the fish drives up the vertical frame. |
| 1210 | Jaws, macro tracking | Hold the approach briefly in slow motion, then accelerate into contact on the existing strike cue. |
| 1247 | Impact aftermath, dolly out | The camera gives ground as bubbles rise, the school scatters and plants recoil. |
| 1339 | Uncertainty, wide orbit | The pike retreats into cover while the narrator questions the motive. |
| 1480 | Conditions, school tracking | Shift attention to the rest of the habitat. The minnows settle into separate paths. |
| 1579 | Callback, dolly with rack focus | Return to a new pause and twitch. Hold the question on the fish and lure. |

The edit resolves spoken anchors from the checked-in WhisperX timings with a
two-frame lead. Surface entry and contact also follow the fixed soundtrack cues.
Some motions start before their cue so the viewer can read the anticipation.
