# Storyboard — 30 fps, sixty seconds

| Frames | Shot | Action / focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0–170 | Hidden hunter | Pike and lure, weed-edge ambush; hook immediately questioned |
| 171–245 | One cast | Original angler on bank, rod pivot at hand, ballistic lure and landing ripple |
| 246–380 | Through the surface | Camera descends under sunlit water, establishes weed bed |
| 381–620 | Wobble and flash | Close lure, oscillating body and flank rotation |
| 621–860 | Water motion | Pike flank, line, suspended particles and current |
| 861–1100 | Follow without biting | Lure stops; pike turns and holds |
| 1101–1340 | Load and launch | Tail preparation, short accelerating lunge, jaw opening |
| 1341–1580 | Uncertainty | Wider ecosystem, no invented motivational diagram |
| 1581–1799 | Clues not commands | New lure observation, quiet callback and comment invitation |

Word events come from `timing/words.json`, measured against the completed dry
Kokoro narration. Events are rounded to 30 fps, then anchor two frames early; preparation curves may begin earlier than
the anchor (tail loading and jaw anticipation).
Exact sound events (seconds): `timing/events.json`. The splash-like underwater
strike is stylized; it is not presented as an air splash deep underwater.
